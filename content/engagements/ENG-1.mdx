A structured look at your company through the tools we have today: how AI
systems describe you, when they recommend you, what proof they ask for, and
where their confidence in you visibly breaks down. We tell you how many
tests we ran and how much the results varied.

receive evidence and a prioritized set of decisions, not a promise about
what any AI system will do next.

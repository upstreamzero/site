## Research question

Does the committee structure live in the category or in the evaluator?

## Why it mattered

The highest-consequence run to that point: every hypothesis rested on a
single evaluator. Running the frozen protocol on a second evaluator tested
whether the structure transfers.

## Method

Evaluator: ChatGPT, logged out, incognito, browsing on. The frozen four-turn
protocol, identical to E-002's baseline. Logged out to avoid months of
Upstream Zero project context contaminating the evaluator. Sample: one draw.

## Direct observations

- A compliance-led committee structure appeared on a second evaluator, same
  category.
- Solution taxonomy diverged: architecture-first on Google, ecosystem and
  organization-type-first on ChatGPT.
- An explicit requirement matching an active implicit gate produced no
  compression.

## Interpretation (not established)

Committee structure may be category-borne rather than evaluator-borne, on one
category and one run each, which is very weak evidence. Evaluators may differ
more in how they carve the market than in what they gate on, a reading later
weakened by E-009. On a re-read after the E-014 narration-and-reasoning
split, ChatGPT's reasoning was in fact dense across all five owners
(economic, operations, technical, user, compliance) with near-zero naming,
which softens the "ChatGPT ties weaker" claim to a difference in narration
style rather than in reasoning.

## Outcome and remaining uncertainty

Supported: the compliance-led committee structure replicated on a second
evaluator. The scope is narrow and partial. One prediction missed under
strict scoring, one was partial, and n=1 on each evaluator. The transfer is a
replication of structure, not a general law.

## Evidence and methods limitations

Raw outputs for this run are unrecoverable; they were captured as pasted text
during a live session and only the coding survives. Single evaluator per arm,
one draw, single coder.

## Contribution to the research program

C-01 (gate saturation), C-02 (evaluator-specific segmentation), and C-03
(disclosed withheld assumptions) were born here.

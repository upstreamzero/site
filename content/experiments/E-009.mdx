## Research question

How much does the recommendation move on its own, with no variable changed?

## Why it mattered

Flip magnitudes are meaningless without a reference. This run established the
variance band so movement could be measured against something. It is the
program's first ruler.

## Method

Evaluator: ChatGPT, logged out, incognito, fresh session per draw. A single
prompt, "Best CDP for healthcare?", one response, no follow-ups. Sample: five
clean draws. A sixth was struck as a cache duplicate: the original third draw
was character-identical to the second and was not counted.

## Direct observations

- Five draws of an identical prompt produced a stable vendor core, a
  semi-stable periphery, and a noisy tail.
- Ordering within the core varied across draws with no variable changed.
- Two apparently separate sessions returned a byte-identical response.

## Interpretation (not established)

The recommendation surface has strata, and measuring movement requires
knowing which stratum moved. Consumer AI surfaces may cache commercial
recommendation responses, meaning some buyers asking the same question in the
same window receive literally the same generation rather than similar draws
from a distribution. The mechanism is unconfirmed, whether platform cache,
CDN, or A-B serving.

## Outcome and remaining uncertainty

Baseline Complete: a valid reference measurement. The band is a measured
property of exactly one condition (ChatGPT, healthcare CDP, logged out) and
is not assumed to transfer. Core membership was stable across draws; core
ordering was not.

## Evidence and methods limitations

Raw outputs for this run are unrecoverable; only the coding survives. The
band holds for one condition only. The caching observation is a single
incident with an unconfirmed mechanism.

## Contribution to the research program

Produced the three-strata band model and formalized the flip-scoring rule:
real movement is a core-membership change, a gate-framing or checklist
change, or category dissolution, while core reordering and periphery churn
sit within the band. Logged O-001 (caching), whose characterization is queued
as the proposed experiment E-012.

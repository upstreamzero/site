## Research question

Do twenty-five years of practitioner priors predict machine evaluation in a
category the practitioner actually sold?

## Why it mattered

A maximum-strength test of practitioner prediction in a depreciating asset:
uncontaminated operator knowledge. If the first-order model transfers, the
extraction is reading something a domain expert recognizes.

## Method

Evaluator: Google AI Mode, signed in, retrieval on. A frozen four-turn
sequence: "Best first aid and safety services company?", then a manufacturer
with twelve plants and OSHA recordkeeping, then AED program management with
compliance tracking, then extraction. Sample: one draw. The card included,
for the first time, a pre-committed divergence guess with a cause type.

## Direct observations

- The cluster, three gates, and kill order predicted from deal memory all
  landed.
- Route density, an operational capability, appeared explicitly in the
  implicit-requirement set.
- Execution-quality variance appeared nowhere across four turns.
- Gate three was architected around corporate safety directors versus local
  plant managers, two named owners with distinct needs, coded as a present
  committee tie.

## Interpretation (not established)

The practitioner's first-order model transfers. The practitioner's model of
the machine's blind spot was one revision too coarse. The ghost class refined
from "operational" to "operational and undocumented."

## Outcome and remaining uncertainty

Supported: first-order predictions landed near ceiling. The ghost prediction
failed, but productively: route density did appear, so the blind spot is not
"operational" but "operational and undocumented." What the evaluator still
did not surface anywhere was execution-quality variance.

## Evidence and methods limitations

Single evaluator, one draw, and a single coder with direct operator priors in
this category, which is a source of both signal and bias.

## Contribution to the research program

Refined C-06 (accumulation channel, not knowledge type) and produced C-08
(the quality-variance blind spot).

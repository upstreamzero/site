## Research question

Can a single requirement be decomposed into the full causal chain beneath
it?

## Why it mattered

Turn-one extraction shows the requirements. This run asked whether one
requirement can be pushed down through the business problem, workflow,
failure modes, owners, and consequences that produce it.

## Method

Evaluator: Google AI Mode, signed in, retrieval on. Starting from "Why is
matter code integration important?", followed by decomposition follow-ups.
Sample: one draw. No card was frozen; the thirteen-question battery was
designed only after this run showed the decomposition worked.

## Direct observations

- One requirement decomposed into business problem, workflow, failure modes,
  owners, and consequences across three prompts.
- Consequence-severity framing appeared without prompting.
- Owner multiplicity (finance and ethics or bar) was visible at depth but
  not at turn one.
- Committee ownership was referenced across the consequence chain, coded as a
  partial committee tie.

## Interpretation (not established)

Decomposition depth is a variable that changes what the instrument can see.
The operational substrate surfaces because evaluation is downstream of
operations, or because the model generates plausible operational detail on
demand. This run is assessment-adjacent yet still carried owner references,
so it does not cleanly separate from evaluation mode; mode may interact with
decomposition depth.

## Outcome and remaining uncertainty

Supported: one requirement did decompose into its full causal chain. The
scope is narrow. Fidelity at operational depth is untested and carries a
higher verification bar than evaluation-grade claims. Whether the
operational detail is read or generated on demand is unresolved.

## Evidence and methods limitations

Single evaluator, one draw, single coder, no codebook. Operational-fidelity
claims (logged as RQ-001) are explicitly parked behind the evaluation
program at a higher evidence bar.

## Contribution to the research program

Produced the thirteen-question decomposition battery as a method, and logged
RQ-001 (implementation fidelity).

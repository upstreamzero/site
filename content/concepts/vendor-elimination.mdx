Vendor elimination studies the moment a company leaves the recommendation set:
which requirement removes it, and whether the removal holds across repeated
runs. A real elimination is one where a vendor is present, then absent, and
stays absent.

The core question is where and why a vendor is dropped. What remains open is
whether the same elimination appears on more than one evaluator, and how much
of it traces to the requirement rather than the evaluator.

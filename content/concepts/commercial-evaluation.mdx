This is the commercial evaluation concept as it appears in the research graph.
For the full, canonical definition of commercial evaluation, and how
AI-mediated commercial evaluation works, see the reference page:
[Commercial Evaluation](/learn/commercial-evaluation).

On the research side, commercial evaluation is the invariant the program
studies: the process by which a buyer, or a system acting for one, narrows a
field of vendors into a recommendation and a selection. Each research component,
recommendation set formation, vendor elimination, frontrunner movement,
recommendation survivability, validation and evidence, and the rest, studies a
part of it. Underneath every one of those decisions are
[requirements](/concepts/requirements).

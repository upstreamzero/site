Selection survivability studies what happens to a company once a buyer stops
asking a generic question and starts adding real requirements. A field can
hold, collapse to a single vendor, survive while its members are replaced, or
be wiped out and relocated to a different market tier.

The core question is whether a company remains recommended as requirements
accumulate, and if the field survives, whether the same vendors survive with
it. This is the component the recommendation survivability runs were built to
measure.

What remains open is what property of a category or requirement produces which
survival behavior, and whether the same behavior holds across evaluators.

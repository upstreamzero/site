Validation and evidence studies what an evaluator appears to lean on when it
decides whether a company credibly meets a requirement: the sources it cites,
the kinds of proof it treats as sufficient, and the trust signals that move a
vendor from mentioned to recommended.

The core question is which evidence an evaluator appears to use during
validation. What remains open is how much of the observed narration reflects
the actual mechanism, which is why claims here are held at their evidence tier.

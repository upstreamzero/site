Recommendation stability studies whether the same question and the same
requirements produce the same recommendation across repeated runs. When
outcomes move, the instability may sit in the recommendation itself or upstream
in how the evaluator associates a requirement with a vendor.

The core question is how stable recommendation outcomes are, and where any
instability originates. What remains open is how much variation is normal and
what separates unstable requirement association from an unstable recommendation.

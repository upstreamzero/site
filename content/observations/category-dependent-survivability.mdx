Across five categories under the same requirement battery, the recommendation
behaved in at least four distinguishable ways when requirements were added in
sequence:

- Collapse to a single incumbent, in clinical trial management software
  ([E-034](/experiments/E-034)).
- Survival with stable membership, in project management software
  ([E-040](/experiments/E-040)) and video conferencing
  ([E-045](/experiments/E-045)).
- Survival with near-total membership churn, in CRM software
  ([E-041](/experiments/E-041)).
- Wipeout of the named field and migration to a higher tier, in accounting
  software ([E-043](/experiments/E-043)).

These are recorded as observed classes, not as a settled taxonomy. How to code
the accounting case, and whether the two survival modes are one class or two,
are open judgment calls. Most runs used a single evaluator, and nothing here
shows what property of a category produces which behavior.

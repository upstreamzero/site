Across five categories, the vendor that led the opening recommendation lost its
lead at the first requirement it structurally could not satisfy. The removal
appeared to target the leading position rather than a particular vendor: in CRM
([E-041](/experiments/E-041)) the leader changed between draws, HubSpot in one
and Salesforce in the others, yet whichever vendor led was removed at the same
step in every draw.

The pattern took different forms. In some categories the leader was eliminated
at the first requirement; in accounting ([E-043](/experiments/E-043)) it
survived one requirement and was removed at the second; in video conferencing
([E-045](/experiments/E-045)) it was demoted rather than eliminated. It is held,
not promoted. The runs above used mostly one evaluator, and whether the same
holds across evaluators is an open question.

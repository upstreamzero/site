On the same category and the same requirement, the two evaluators took opposite
stances on one claim. One asserted that a single product met the requirement
natively and funneled the recommendation to it. The other stated that no
product met the requirement natively, that all relied on connectors, and kept a
broad set of products at the end. This was consistent across the draws in
[E-044](/experiments/E-044) and its companion [E-043](/experiments/E-043).

This is recorded as an observation about the evaluators' stated rationales, not
about which stance is correct. It is held, not promoted, from a single category
on two evaluators. Whether this posture difference recurs in other categories
is an open question.

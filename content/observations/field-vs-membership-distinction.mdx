Two survival runs behaved differently. In CRM ([E-041](/experiments/E-041)) the
field survived but its members were almost entirely replaced turn by turn. In
video conferencing ([E-045](/experiments/E-045)) the field survived with its
original members and reordered them. So survival appeared in two modes, stable
membership and total churn.

The cause is not attributed here. The two runs also differed in the kind of
requirement used: video conferencing used capability-framed requirements and
CRM used pricing and packaging-framed requirements. Market maturity and
requirement framing move together across these two experiments, so the
difference cannot be assigned to either one. The existence of the two modes is
registered; the cause is left open, and a dedicated experiment would be needed
to separate them.
